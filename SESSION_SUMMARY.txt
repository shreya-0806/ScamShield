   Fix: Removed entire loop, rely on async callbacks
   Location: VoskProcessor.java:initModelAsync()

✅ 4. HANDLER TASKS NOT CLEANED (HIGH PRIORITY)
   Problem: Polling task continues after service destroyed (NPE)
   Fix: Store Runnable reference, cancel in onDestroy()
   Location: ScamMonitorService.java:63, 510-520

✅ 5. JSON PARSING SILENT FAILURES (MEDIUM PRIORITY)
